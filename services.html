Our Services

CampusCare presents the core student clinic services in a clear and easy-to-understand format.

General Consultation

For common health complaints, basic medical checks, fever, headaches, stomach discomfort, and general wellness guidance.

Opening Hours: Mon - Fri, 8:00 AM - 4:00 PM

Dental Care

For tooth pain, gum complaints, dental hygiene advice, and student dental checkup requests.

Opening Hours: Tue - Thu, 9:00 AM - 2:00 PM

Eye Care

For eye strain, irritation, vision screening requests, and guidance on when further eye care is needed.

Opening Hours: Mon - Wed, 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM

Emergency Support

For urgent clinic attention, sudden illness, minor injuries, and situations that need quick student support.

Opening Hours: Daily, 24/7 Support
